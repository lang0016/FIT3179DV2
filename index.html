A business is not necessarily one farm.

The people behind the numbers

Farming has a distinctly older occupational workforce.

04
SECTORS

There is no typical farm business

Agriculture includes very different businesses, from nurseries to cattle farms.

Compare regional specialisation, annual sales and staffing. These measure different aspects of a business.

Highlights charts 7–9; every sector stays visible.
05
PERSPECTIVE

Look beyond the size of the land

Land area can hide where businesses are concentrated.

Changing the map reveals dairy’s Victorian centre. Staffing and workforce trends add another perspective.

WHAT TO TAKE AWAY

One industry.
Many different stories.

Australian farming is not one uniform workforce or business model.

Dairy’s registered farm count has fallen. Farmers skew older, and business structures differ between sectors.

Keep the definitions in view. They change what a number can tell us.
